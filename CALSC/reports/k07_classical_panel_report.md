## 3. Key Findings & The Resolution Argument

- **Per-Fold Inspection:**
  Every model's per-fold recall, precision, macro F1, and confusion matrix is documented without hiding individual fold variance behind medians.
- **Fixed 20 on CALCE vs NASA:**
  On NASA, LOCO warning recall collapsed to **0.0000 across all folds** (0.0591 class-balanced median). On CALCE with Fixed 20, models achieve significant warning recall recovery.
- **Proportional 12% on CALCE:**
  When the warning horizon matches NASA's proportional span (~60-70 cycles), warning recall reaches 50-78% across tree models and linear models with 0/4 zero-recall folds.
