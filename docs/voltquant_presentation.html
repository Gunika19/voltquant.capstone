CRITICAL FLAW

The 72-Second Runaway Cliff Paradox

In degraded battery cells, the discharge curve stays stable until ~45 minutes, then accelerates. Once voltage drops past 3.00 V, the collapse to the 2.75 V cutoff takes only 62 to 89 seconds (mean: 72 seconds).

Why Standard BMS Fails: Reactive alarms triggering at 3.0V give under 90 seconds to escape. Passengers are trapped before thermal runaway intervention is possible.
The VoltQuant Solution: Fires advance alerts at minute 34.0 (15 min buffer) and minute 39.0 (10 min buffer) when cell voltage is still nominal (3.51V - 3.54V).
SCIENTIFIC PIVOT

Why We Dropped NASA PCoE for CALCE

Many academic papers claim >95% accuracy on NASA PCoE. Our audit proved this is an artifact of data leakage:

  • Batch Leakage: Different batteries were tested at different temperatures (4°C, 24°C, 44°C). Random 80/20 splits allowed models to memorize battery serial IDs.
  • 100% LOCO Failure: On unseen batteries, every model collapsed to 0.00% recall on NASA.
  • Short Lifespans: NASA cells died in 132-168 cycles; CALCE cells undergo 900+ cycles under continuous 0.5C discharge, enabling true early prediction.
